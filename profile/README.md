# RTST

**A music streaming platform built for independent artists to sell directly to fans.**

---

## What is RTST?

RTST gives independent musicians the tools to build sustainable careers on their own terms. Artists can stream their music, build memberships, and connect directly with their audience — no middlemen, no gatekeepers.

### For Artists

- **Stream your music** — Upload and deliver high-quality audio and video to your fans
- **Build memberships** — Offer tiered memberships so fans can support you directly
- **Understand your audience** — Real-time analytics to see who's listening and where
- **Get paid** — Integrated billing so you earn from day one

### For Fans

- **Discover independent music** — Find artists who are building something real
- **Support directly** — Your money goes to the artists you love
- **Listen anywhere** — Available on web, iOS, and Android

## Platform

RTST is available at [rtst.app](https://rtst.app) and on mobile for iOS and Android.
